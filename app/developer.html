Past Play

The court each player was on every past Sunday of the season; * = brought the balls. Balls = times they brought them this season. The Scheduler uses this table to choose ball-bringers and to spot groups who played together before, so corrections here change its choices. Locked weeks are added automatically.

In a cell, type the court number (1 to 7), with * if they brought the balls (e.g. 3*). Clear the cell if they didn't play. Each change is saved right away.
