Past Play

The court each player was on every Sunday of the season; * = brought the balls, NS = no-show. Balls = times they brought them this season. Each week is added from the Scheduler once its courts are final (locked, at 8pm Saturday), and can't be changed here. The Scheduler uses this table to choose ball-bringers and to spot groups who played together before; no-shows are left out.

Click a player's court on a Sunday to mark them as a no-show; click it again to undo. Uploaded seasons can't be changed here: fix the file and upload it again.
Upload a past season

For seasons before the app. Use the club's Past Play sheet layout, saved as a CSV file or a tab-separated .txt file: column 1 = player name, then one column per Sunday with the date as its heading (e.g. 5/4, 05-04-2025 or May 4, 2025). In each cell put the court number, with * if they brought the balls (3*) and NS for a no-show (3 NS); leave it empty if they didn't play. Other columns are ignored. Uploading the same season again replaces it.
