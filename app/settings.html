Message for Inactive members

Inactive members can still sign in, but see only this message until a developer marks them Active again (Developers > Members).

Invitation email

Sent when a developer clicks Send Invite in the Members table. It can't be switched off: it's how members join the app.

Weekly schedule (US Eastern)

When each deadline and timed email happens. The conditions are fixed; days and times can be changed (switch emails on or off under Email settings). Changes apply from next week, or from this week if its sign-ups haven't opened yet. If you move a deadline, also update any email wording that mentions it (for example "noon on Saturday").

Sunday Doubles emails

Switch an email Off to stop sending it altogether (it's On unless you switch it off). Words in {{double braces}} are filled in when the email is sent; each email lists the ones it can use. Your edits are used from the next email onwards. Reset to default brings back the original wording.

Wait before emailing players

When players' times line up, wait this long after a time is posted before emailing anyone about it. A player who posts a time by mistake can take it back first, and nobody hears about it.

Casual Play emails

The emails players get when their times line up. Switch one Off to stop sending it altogether.
