STA Members App

One place for club news, Sunday Doubles and Casual Play,
on any phone or computer.

A tour for STA leadership. Every screen shown is the real app, filled with made-up players.

What it does

Everything members need, in one app

News

Club announcements, posted by the organizers.

Sunday Doubles

For approved advanced players: sign up each week, see your court, and review your game.

Casual Play

Post when you're free and get introduced to players at your level who are free too.

Developers

For organizers: members, the court Scheduler, emails and settings.

  • Works in any web browser, on phones and computers. Nothing to install.
  • Members sign in with their email and a password. Only Active members on the club list can use it; Sunday Doubles is only for players the program manager approves.
  • Reminders, court assignments and Casual Play introductions go out by email automatically. Casual Play contact details are only shared between players who agree to share them.
  • Matches the look of statennis.com, with a link back to the club website.
Part 1

For members

What a member sees and does, week to week.

Members

Signing in

  • Each member gets an invitation email and chooses a password.
  • Only members on the club list can get an account, and only while their membership is Active. There is no public sign-up.
  • Members whose dues aren't paid can sign in but see only a reminder to renew.
  • "Forgot password" sends a reset link by email.
Members

News

  • The first page after signing in.
  • Organizers post announcements; links to the club website are clickable.
Sunday Doubles · email

Every Monday: sign-ups open

  • At 9am Monday every approved Sunday Doubles player gets this email, with a link straight to the sign-up page.
  • It also asks last week's players to review their game.
  • The wording of every email can be changed by the organizers, in the app.

Sunday Doubles

Signing up for Sunday

  • Sunday Doubles appears only for players approved by the program manager.
  • One tap to sign up, one tap to cancel, until noon Saturday.
  • Spots go in the order people sign up. When the courts are full, later sign-ups join a waitlist.
Sunday Doubles · email

Saturday 8pm: court assignments

  • Every player with a spot gets their court, their courtmates, and who brings the balls (*).
  • The waitlist is listed in order, so players who can't come can find a replacement.
  • Anyone left without a spot gets a friendly "no spot this week" email with their waitlist place.

Court Assignments page

  • The same courts are on the app, from Saturday 8pm until play.

Sunday Doubles

Game Review

  • After play, each player can record the sets they played, their partner in each set, and the scores.
  • Opponents are filled in automatically from the court.
  • "Rate my game" asks how much they enjoyed it, with optional comments.
  • Players see only their own reviews; organizers see all of them.
Casual Play

Casual Play: who's free?

  • A calendar of the next 4 weeks, 6am to 9pm, showing when other members want to play.
  • Members choose the 1 or 2 levels they want to see. Darker purple means more players are free.
  • Members choose whether to show their name, and whether to share their contact info for games. Others always see sex and level.
  • An orange ring on your own time means a possible singles game; green means a possible doubles game.
Casual Play

Hover to see who

  • Hovering over a time shows each player who is free then: name (if shown), sex and level.
  • On your own times, it also says whether a singles or doubles game is possible.
Casual Play

Posting a time

  • Click any hour or half hour for a 1-hour box; stretch it to 1.5 or 2 hours.
  • Choose whether to show your name, and Yes or No for "Share my contact info for games", for this time.
  • The panel shows who else is already free then.
Casual Play

Setting up a game

  • Clicking a ringed time lists the possible games then: players free for at least an hour with you, at levels that suit both. Doubles needs three such players who also suit each other.
  • Email addresses only go between players who share their contact info. When everyone in a game shares, clicking the ring copies their addresses, ready to write.
  • If you share and some players don't, the app can email them about the game, with the details of those who share. Nothing about them is passed on.
  • Unsubmit takes the time back, for example once a game is set up.
Casual Play · email

Casual Play introductions by email

  • Members with "Email me" on hear when times line up. Players who share their contact info get each other's names and email addresses, and decide together who plays.
  • Players who don't share are asked to, so nobody's details are passed on without their say-so.
  • When a player asks the app to, it emails the players in a game who don't share (right).

Part 2

For developers

The organizers' tools: members, the weekly Scheduler, emails and settings.

Developers

Two-step sign-in

  • Developer pages can change everyone's data, so they need a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on the developer's phone, as well as the password.
  • Set up once by scanning a code. Members are not affected.
Developers · Members

The Members Table

  • One row per person: Name, Email, Membership (Active or Inactive), Sunday Doubles (Yes or No) and App Access, plus level, sex and developer.
  • Change Membership or Sunday Doubles right in the table. Inactive members are locked out until marked Active again.
  • App Access shows who has joined: new members are "Pending STA Member App Invitation" until a developer clicks Send Invite.
Developers · Members

Adding a member

  • Enter the name, email, level and sex, and choose Membership and Sunday Doubles.
  • Then click Send Invite (or Send Invite to all pending) and the member gets an email to set their password.
Developers · Members

Upload the current member list

  • Upload the club's membership export (CSV). Members are matched by email or name.
  • The app shows exactly what will change before anything happens. Members not on the list are marked Inactive and see only the dues reminder until they renew.
Developers · Sunday Program

This week at a glance

  • The Developers area has four tabs: Members, Sunday Program, Scheduler and Settings.
  • Sunday Program shows the current Sunday: sign-up and lock deadlines, and when each email goes out.
  • The number of courts can be changed for the week.
Developers · Sunday Program

This week's line-up

  • Everyone who signed up, in order, with spots and waitlist.
  • A developer can remove a player if needed (recorded as a developer removal).
Developers · Scheduler

Auto-Assign Courts

  • Saturday at noon sign-ups close and the developers get a reminder to arrange the courts.
  • One click arranges everyone with a spot onto courts: same-sex, mixed, or by level.
  • Each court is checked (OK or Needs fix). Players can be swapped by tapping two names.
  • It warns about groups that played together recently.
Developers · Scheduler

Who brings the balls

  • Ball-bringers are chosen fairly, from this season's history.
  • "Modify bring-ball assignments" shows a star for each time a player brought balls this season; red if they brought them last Sunday, pink the Sunday before. Tap a player to choose them instead.
Developers · Scheduler

Lock, and the app does the rest

  • Locking the courts schedules the 8pm emails to every player. They can still be unlocked and changed until then.
  • If rain is expected, choose a weather option instead and everyone signed up is told.
  • If nobody locks in time, the developers are alerted and no wrong emails go out.
Developers · Scheduler

Upload my players

  • The same Scheduler works for any group, not just Sunday Doubles: paste names, levels and sexes, or upload a file.
  • Auto-assign, swaps, ball-bringers, then copy or print the courts.
Developers · Settings

Settings

  • App settings: app name, color, logo, club website and the invitation email.
  • Sunday Doubles Settings: Time settings (the day and time of every deadline and email) and Email settings.
  • Casual Play Settings: the waiting time and the three Casual Play emails.
  • App Messages: the wording of the messages members see on the pages.
Developers · Settings · Sunday Doubles

Every email can be edited, or switched off

  • Each email shows when it is sent and to whom, with an On/Off switch (On unless a developer switches it off).
  • Change the wording, preview it with sample details, or reset to the original. All emails can be printed as one document for review.
Developers · Settings · Casual Play

Casual Play Settings

  • Emails about players lining up wait until both times have been posted for a while (2 hours by default).
  • A member who posts a time by mistake can take it back before anyone is emailed.
  • The Casual Play email wording is here too, with its own On/Off switches.
Developers · Settings · App Messages

App Messages

  • Every message members see on the app's pages can be reworded here: for example what Inactive members see, or the note for members not approved for Sunday Doubles.
  • Grouped by where each message appears, with Reset to default.
Developers

Posting news

  • Developers post, edit and delete News announcements right on the News page.
How a week runs

The Sunday Doubles week, automatically

  1. Mon 9:00amSign-ups open; every member gets the invite email.
  2. Mon–SatMembers sign up or cancel. The waitlist moves up automatically, with an email to anyone who gets a spot.
  3. Sat 12:00pmSign-ups close. Developers are reminded to run the Scheduler.
  4. Sat afternoonA developer arranges and locks the courts (reminders at 4pm and 7pm if not yet done).
  5. Sat 8:00pmCourt emails to every player, "no spot" emails to the waitlist; the Court Assignments page opens.
  6. Sun 9–10:30amPlay.
  7. After playPlayers review their game until the next Sunday.

All times can be changed in the app. Casual Play runs all year, with emails as soon as times line up.

Privacy and security

Built to keep members' details safe

  • Only invited, Active members can use the app, and only approved players see Sunday Doubles. Each member sees only what they need: other players' names on the courts, and on Casual Play only what each player chooses to show.
  • Email addresses are shared only between Casual Play players who both chose to show their name.
  • The rules are enforced by the database itself, not just the pages, and have been through a security review.
  • Developers need two-step sign-in. Passwords are handled by a professional sign-in service (Supabase) and stored only in scrambled form.
  • We collect only name, email, level, sex and play history. The invite email explains what is stored and how to ask for removal.
Next steps

What it takes to go live

Email from statennis.com

The club's Wix administrator adds 3–4 email records, about 15 minutes, so emails come from mail.statennis.com and don't land in spam. The website and club email are untouched.

Running costs

Free to start: hosting, database and email all have free plans. An optional $25/month database plan adds automatic daily backups.

Roll-out

Load the Members Table and mark the Sunday Doubles players, invite a few members to try it, then invite everyone. Volunteers run it from the Developers pages; no programming needed week to week.
